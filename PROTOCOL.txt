Wire format (observed statically, corroborated by the live info/status queries):
  51 78 | command | direction | payload length (uint16 LE) | payload | CRC | FF
CRC-8: polynomial 0x07, initial 0, no reflection or final XOR, payload only.
Direction 00 is host request; 01 is printer response.
Commands used:
  A4: darkness preset, payload 33.
  AF: print energy, uint16 LE; default 5000.
  BE: monochrome image mode, payload 00.
  BD: motor speed, payload 0A for image rows, 19 for tear-off feed.
  A2: raw raster row, 48 bytes, black=1, leftmost pixel=least-significant bit.
  A1: paper feed, uint16 LE dots; app's two 48-dot feeds combined into 96.
  A3: status query, payload 00.
  A8: device information query, payload 00.
  AE response 10: pause writes; response 00: resume.
A3 status byte: 01 paper out, 02 cover open, 04 overheating, 08 low battery,
  10 charging, 80 printing. The driver checks every error bit independently.

Design choices, not Android behavior:
  CUPS PDF filter + root-run RFCOMM backend, nominal 200 DPI, paper centered.
  Fixed-width pages, trailing blank trimming, bounded energy/feed settings.
  Raw A2 rows rather than app RLE/LZO or grayscale paths.
  No identity rewriting, account activation, firmware updates, or app assets.

Unknown until tested separately:
  Other printer models/firmware, grayscale, gap labels, battery calibration,

  printing were confirmed for the tested X6h, not for other hardware models.
